## Problem

A small business that serves its customers on WhatsApp needs quick replies. The landing page has to show clearly how the service works before anyone signs up.

## Solution

- I designed the landing page for a WhatsApp customer service SaaS with AI, aimed at small businesses.
- I included a chat demo for each industry, so visitors can see the service before buying it.
- I added a pricing section.
- I built it with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, without a framework.

## Outcome

The landing page is finished and the chat demo runs in the browser, with no backend.
