## Problem

People who are starting in mobile programming need a clear path, one step at a time. University students, in turn, need focused help with their Android and Data Structures courses.

## Solution

- I teach online classes on mobile app development from scratch, using App Inventor, Dart and Flutter.
- I prepare the material for each class as a PDF, with my own explanations and examples.
- I support university students in Android, with Kotlin, Room and Firestore.
- I support Data Structures in C, covering trees and binary search trees.

## Outcome

I have 24 lessons of my own material in PDF.
